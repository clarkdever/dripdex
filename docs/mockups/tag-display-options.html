Adult Science Draft

House Finches are small seed-eating songbirds often seen around feeders, yards, open woodland edges, and urban spaces. Males commonly show red to orange-red color on the head and chest, while females are brown and streaked.

Identification Clues

Look for a short conical bill, streaked belly, bouncing flight, and musical warbling song. Similar species may include Purple Finch or House Sparrow, depending on location and plumage.
